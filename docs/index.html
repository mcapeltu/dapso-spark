Distributed asynchronous PSO for irregular machine-learning workloads

DAPSO-Spark investigates application-level batch scheduling for particle-based optimisation on Apache Spark, with classification, regression and scaling experiments plus reproducibility scripts.

Project overview

The project studies the performance behaviour of Particle Swarm Optimisation when fitness evaluations are distributed through Spark. DAPSO groups particles into fixed-size batches and redispatches work as execution capacity becomes available.

DAPSO

Asynchronous, batch-oriented particle evaluation designed to reduce waiting caused by irregular evaluation times.

Baselines

Sequential PSO and experimental configurations for direct runtime and predictive-quality comparison.

Evaluation

Classification, regression and dataset-size experiments with repeated runs, confidence intervals and speedup analysis.
